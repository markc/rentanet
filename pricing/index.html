Plan Details

Every Container Is the Whole Stack

Web, mail, DNS, CMS and PIM in every tier — the only thing that changes is capacity

Lite

  • $79/year — about $6.60/month
  • 1 vCPU / 1GB RAM / 10GB NVMe
  • Your domain, done properly
  • Site + real mail + DNS

The full Cosmix stack for a personal domain or side project: your website and blog, real mailboxes at your own domain, authoritative DNS, contacts and calendar. The all-Rust stack idles in a few hundred megabytes, so 1GB is genuinely comfortable — this is a complete online presence, not a starter compromise.

Standard

  • $139/year — about $11.60/month
  • 2 vCPU / 2GB RAM / 20GB NVMe
  • Small business daily driver
  • A few domains, a small team

Headroom for a small business: several domains, mailboxes for the whole team, a busier site with the built-in CMS and blog, shared contacts and calendars. The sweet spot for most customers moving off cPanel-era hosting or a tangle of SaaS subscriptions.

Plus

  • $239/year — about $19.90/month
  • 3 vCPU / 4GB RAM / 40GB NVMe
  • Multi-domain, heavier mail
  • Forum and file sharing

For agencies and busy operators: a stable of client domains, heavier mail flow, the forum and file-manager surfaces in real use, and room for large mail archives. Add $20 units as individual domains grow instead of re-platforming anything.

Max

  • $419/year — about $34.90/month
  • 4 vCPU / 8GB RAM / 80GB NVMe
  • Consolidate everything
  • Priority support included

One container that replaces a rack's worth of subscriptions: every domain, every mailbox, all your sites and archives in a single managed place with serious headroom. Compare it to an unmanaged 4-core/8GB VPS at around $470/year — Max costs less and includes full Claude Code management.
