Extremely Linux Friendly

We don't just host Linux — we run it on every desktop we own. If you want the same at home or in the office, we recommend and fully support CachyOS with KDE Plasma, from installation to ongoing maintenance.

Why CachyOS

Arch-based with x86-64-v3/v4 optimised, PGO and BOLT enhanced packages — measurably faster than generic distributions. ZFS is available straight from the installer, and there's no telemetry and no mandatory accounts.

Escaping Windows 11

Windows 11 hardware requirements leave millions of perfectly good machines behind. CachyOS runs beautifully on that hardware, KDE Plasma feels instantly familiar, and you get complete privacy and control. Typical installation takes 30–60 minutes on virtually any Windows-compatible machine from the last 20 years.
