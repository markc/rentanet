## Managed hosting plans

Managed Linux containers on Proxmox with NVMe storage and the native MixOS
service stack: web, mail, DNS and content management in one place. Annual
prices in AUD.

| Plan     | vCPU | Memory | NVMe  | Price     |
|----------|------|--------|-------|-----------|
| Lite     | 1    | 1 GB   | 10 GB | $79/year  |
| Standard | 2    | 2 GB   | 20 GB | $139/year |
| Plus     | 3    | 4 GB   | 40 GB | $239/year |
| Max      | 4    | 8 GB   | 80 GB | $419/year |

Pricing is one formula: a $19/year base plus $20/year per resource unit. A
unit is 1 vCPU, 1 GB of memory or 10 GB of NVMe storage. Lite is 3 units,
Standard 6, Plus 11 and Max 20. Capacity can be added one unit at a time.
Bulk storage and dedicated IPv4 are available on request.

Included in management: OS updates, security patches, SSL certificate
renewals, monitoring, daily backups and email support.

Also available by arrangement: managed Debian virtual machines, and
management of an existing BinaryLane, Vultr or DigitalOcean VPS or an
existing Proxmox cluster.
